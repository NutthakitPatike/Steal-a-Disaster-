# Social content — development updates and discovery

Distribution: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels. Goal: validate a game hook and recruit beta testers, not pretend the unfinished game is launched.

## Repeatable content experiment
- First 1–2 seconds: interesting on-screen gameplay outcome or a surprising disaster.
- Next: one understandable cause/effect and reaction.
- End: ask viewers to choose or comment; link a real beta invite only when available.
- Capture actual Studio/gameplay footage. Label concept renders as concept renders.
- Post the same core clip across platforms with platform-native captions.

## What to measure
- Per-video views are insufficient. Track watch-through, shares, comments, profile visits, beta signups, new players by campaign, first-session completion, next-day return.
- Compare alternate hooks and thumbnails. Keep a log of what changed between clips.
- Do not claim a game is already live or a rarity rate exists unless implemented and verified.

## Low-cost milestone ideas
1. First funny disaster behavior, 5–15 seconds.
2. 3 variant showcase with genuine gameplay.
3. Friend-versus-friend steal and counterplay.
4. Beta playtest reaction and major fix.
5. Weekly new-event teaser only after gameplay is stable.

Always obtain permission before publishing a friend's voice, face, or private chat.
