# Game vision — hypothesis, not a committed final design

**Working title:** Steal a Disaster!
**North-star business goal:** Create a Roblox experience with sustainable player growth, not a game that gets a one-day spike and loses everyone.

## Player promise
Within 10 seconds, a new player should understand what to do and see a funny/visually striking result. Players collect strange disasters, defend their base, and attempt exciting steals from other bases.

## Proposed prototype loop
1. Own one beginner disaster.
2. Place it to earn an understandable in-game resource.
3. Upgrade or discover a second disaster with distinct behavior.
4. Try to steal a disaster from someone else. Clear risk and counterplay must exist.
5. Retain the collection and come back to collect a different result.

## Testable differentiator
Stolen disasters can react in a surprising yet readable way, causing a chain of events involving the thief and owner. Prioritize fair and amusing interactions over unavoidable griefing.

## Vertical slice scope
- One map, 2–4 players in prototype tests, 3 disaster types.
- One currency, one collection/inventory, one claimable plot per player.
- One steal/protect interaction; simple UI and one noticeable visual effect.
- Server-validation and save/load included before public beta.
- Mobile input and frame-time testing required.
- No paid monetization, rebirth, trading, giant worlds, or complex weather systems in this phase.

## Validation
Instrument tutorial completion, first meaningful action, first steal, play duration, next-day return, and crashes/errors. Define thresholds after the first playtests; do not invent success numbers.

## Status
Idea is intentionally provisional until the team playtests and reviews actual player behavior.
